Booking, purchase, financing, and trade-in policy of APEX Sports showroom

1. Booking and delivery terms (same for all APEX Sports models)
- Booking deposit: 50,000 THB per car (full refund if the loan application is not approved under the conditions)
- Payment and delivery deadline: customers must pay the remaining balance and take delivery within 30 days from the booking date
- Trade-in policy: the APEX Sports showroom does not accept used-car trade-ins or exchanges in any case

2. Standard loan terms and interest rates (same for all APEX Sports models)
- Maximum financing: 75% of the car price (minimum down payment 25% of the car price; no zero-down promotion)
- Standard interest rate: starts at 2.09% to 2.79% per year, depending on down payment and installment period
- 48 months (4 years): interest rate 2.09% per year
- 60 months (5 years): interest rate 2.44% per year
- 72 months (6 years): interest rate 2.79% per year
