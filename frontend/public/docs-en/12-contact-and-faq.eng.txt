2. Frequently asked questions (FAQ)
- Q: What fuel can all APEX Sports cars use?
  A: All models support unleaded petrol octane 95 or higher (Petrol 95 or premium Gasohol 95 recommended, and octane 98-100 for track models).
- Q: Can I trade in an old car or get zero down payment?
  A: The showroom does not accept used-car trade-ins in any case, and requires a minimum 25% down payment (up to 75% financing).
- Q: Does the APEX Sports showroom sell electric vehicles (EVs) or hybrids?
  A: The APEX Sports showroom sells only 100% internal combustion engine (ICE) petrol sports cars and supercars across all 10 models, with no EVs or hybrids in any case.
