Test-drive, warranty, and standard service information of APEX Sports

1. Test-drive terms of APEX Sports showroom
- Test-drive hours: open daily, 09:00 - 18:00 at the APEX Sports showroom
- Driver requirements: must be at least 23 years old, hold a valid (unexpired) personal car driving license, and pass basic screening
- Booking: please book at least 1 day in advance; a showroom specialist will ride along throughout the route

2. Standard warranty and service intervals
- Car warranty period (all models): 4 years or 120,000 km (whichever comes first)
- Warranty exclusions: does not cover tires, brake pads, wear-and-tear fluids, and damage from misuse
- Standard service interval (all models): every 10,000 km or every 6 months (whichever comes first), with the first 1,000 km checkup free
